What we speak about

We tailor every engagement, but most talks land in one of these themes:

STEM & possibility

Making aerospace accessible. Representation, pathways into engineering, and why the next generation belongs in the room.

Faith under pressure

Holding belief and ambition together when the work is hard and the stakes are high.

Marriage & mission

Building a life and career as partners without losing each other — or the dream.

Dream it. Build it.

From an 11-year-old’s sketch of a rocket to working on systems that reach deep space.
