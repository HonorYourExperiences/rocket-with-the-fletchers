About

The story behind the mission.

Two engineers. One marriage. A shared call to lift the next generation.

Tiera Fletcher

Tiera earned her B.S. in Aerospace Engineering from MIT and has worked on NASA’s Space Launch System. Her roles have spanned structural design and analysis, product lifecycle, and program leadership.

She is co-author of Wonder Women of Science and the children’s series under Rocket With The Fletchers.

Myron Fletcher

Myron holds degrees in Aerospace Science Engineering and Physics from Tuskegee University. He has served as a rocket propulsion test engineer on NASA’s Space Launch System and in engineering leadership roles.

Why Rocket With The Fletchers exists

The motto is deliberate: Faith comes first. Love is the foundation. Rockets are both the work and the metaphor — proof that what starts as a dream can be designed, tested, and launched.

Through speaking, books, and the podcast, the goal is simple: uplift, encourage, and make ambitious work feel more possible.

Media & press

Features have included Discovery Channel / Science Channel, Thrive Global, Shoutout Atlanta, Voyage ATL, and more.
